Algebraic combinatorics learning seminar: Solvable lattice models in combinatorics

Fall 2026
Organizers: Grant Barkley, Thomas Lam, Yelena Mandelshtam, Rui Xiong

A learning seminar on solvable lattice models in combinatorics. Our goal is to understand (some subset of) the following three papers by Allen Knutson and Paul Zinn-Justin:

  • [1]: Schubert puzzles and integrability I: invariant trilinear forms
  • [2]: Schubert puzzles and integrability II: multiplying motivic Segre classes
  • [3]: Schubert puzzles and integrability III: separated descents

Location:
East Hall 4088

Time:
Wednesdays 2:30-3:30pm

Background:
  • [4]: Six-Vertex, Loop and Tiling models: Integrability and Combinatorics
